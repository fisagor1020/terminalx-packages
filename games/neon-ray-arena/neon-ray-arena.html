How to Play & Difficulty

⚡ Laser Ray Siphoning: Nodes project sweeping laser ray fans. Crossing rival rays siphons health continuously and charges points.

🛡️ Clashing Beams & Defense: When two nodes face each other, their laser fans collide, creating sparks and reducing drain damage. Nodes execute tactical evasion at higher difficulties.

💎 Energy Core Orbs: Pulsing neon orbs spawn in the arena. Nodes can absorb them to replenish HP, enabling thrilling comebacks.

📱 Interactive Controls:
• Tap empty arena: Generates a radial impulse shockwave.
• Drag any node: Sling or maneuver it directly.
• Speed Buttons (1x, 1.5x, 2x, 3x): Change speed dynamically.
